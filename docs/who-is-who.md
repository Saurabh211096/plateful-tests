## Who to ask what

- **"Is this a bug or is it how it should work?"** Check the ticket first. Still unclear: ask Priya, who takes it to Ananya.
- **"Why does this break?"** The developer of that area (Rohan for API and data, Sneha for screens).
- **"Which build is on QA? QA is down."** Amit.
- **"What should I do next?"** Priya.
